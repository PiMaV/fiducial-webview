Assumptions & camera model (per user/camera)

K source: localStorage (calibrate.html) or defaultCamera(w,h). Calibrate camera.

  • Per user/camera: Focal length (fx, fy), distortion (fisheye, etc.), principal point.
  • Tag size LUT: ID 100–199=6mm, 200–299=12mm, 300–399=20mm.
  • R fc: Quaternion SLERP lowpass for rotation. 0=raw, 1 Hz=soft (default), 2 Hz=faster.
  • K-matrix: Calibrate in calibrate.html. Verification: place 3 markers, check distances.
  • Distance: Shows distance between first and second visible marker (3D).
